Help & Information

Our system uses computer vision to track your eye movements and facial features. It calculates the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR), which measures how open or closed your eyes are. When your eyes remain closed or nearly closed for a certain period, the system determines that you're experiencing drowsiness and triggers an alert.

The settings on this page allow you to customize the sensitivity of this detection to match your personal characteristics.

  • EAR Threshold: The Eye Aspect Ratio value that determines when your eyes are considered "closed." Lower values are more lenient.
  • Consecutive Frames: How many consecutive frames your eyes must be closed before triggering an alert. Higher values require longer eye closure.
  • Sensitivity Preset: A combination of settings optimized for different use cases:
    • Low: Minimal alerts, only for significant drowsiness (EAR: 0.2, Frames: 30)
    • Medium: Balanced detection (EAR: 0.25, Frames: 20)
    • High: Early warnings, more alerts (EAR: 0.28, Frames: 15)

Camera not working?

  • Make sure you've granted camera permissions in your browser
  • Try selecting a different camera source if available
  • Close other applications that might be using your camera
  • Refresh the page and try again

False alerts?

  • Adjust the EAR threshold to a lower value
  • Increase the consecutive frames setting
  • Ensure good lighting conditions
  • Remove glasses if possible, or ensure they don't reflect light

Missing alerts?

  • Increase the EAR threshold
  • Decrease the consecutive frames setting
  • Make sure your face is clearly visible to the camera
