Understanding Drowsiness

The Science of Drowsiness

Drowsy driving is responsible for thousands of accidents each year. Understanding the signs can save lives:

Visual Signs
  • Heavy eyelids
  • Frequent blinking
  • Difficulty focusing
  • Nodding head
Cognitive Signs
  • Wandering thoughts
  • Memory lapses
  • Missed exits/turns
  • Slowed reaction time

Our system detects these visual cues before you even realize you're drowsy, providing timely alerts to keep you safe.
