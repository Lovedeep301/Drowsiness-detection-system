How It Works

Drowsiness Detection Technology

Our system uses advanced computer vision and machine learning algorithms to detect signs of drowsiness in real-time:

  • Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR): Measures how open or closed your eyes are
  • Blink Rate Analysis: Tracks changes in your normal blinking pattern
  • Head Pose Estimation: Detects nodding or drooping head movements
  • Real-Time Alerts: Provides immediate audio and visual warnings

The system is designed to help prevent accidents caused by drowsy driving by alerting you before you fall asleep.
